SAP-C03 · the one deck

Professional Cards

Everything for the exam in one place, in four parts: notes at professional depth, the contrast sets organised by deciding axis, a one-line recall pass for every service, and the log and multi-AZ lookup tables. Filter, blank mode and shuffle.

How to use it: run Quick recall once to find the names you can't recall. Then spend your time in the Contrast sets — reproduce a table from blank, check, repeat. Definitions are the cheap part; telling neighbours apart is what the exam grades. Professional notes are filled in as each topic gets studied rather than up front. The Lookup tables are a reference rather than a pass — check them when a question turns on where a log goes, or whether a storage service survives an AZ.

Earlier decks, now folded into the one above and no longer updated: Quick Cards, Concept Cards, Logs and Multi-AZ Cards.
